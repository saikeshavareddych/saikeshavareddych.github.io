How I work

adaptability

The plan changes at hour 30

Requirements move, a provider deprecates an API, a judge changes the rules mid-event. I re-plan without re-panicking, and I would rather rewrite a module than defend one that no longer fits.

pressure

Calm is a technical skill

Under a deadline or an incident, the work is the same: narrow the blast radius, find the last thing that changed, make one change at a time. Speed comes from not thrashing.

invention

Boring fix first, clever fix second

I like an original solution, but only after the obvious one has been ruled out on the evidence. The creative work usually turns out to be in the constraints, not the tooling.

depth

I chase it to the root

I overthink problems, on purpose. A fix I cannot explain the cause of is a fix that comes back. Everything in the builds below has a written reason, including the choices I would make differently now.
